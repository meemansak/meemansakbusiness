About me

I’m Meemansak Bharti.

I’m a filmmaker, post-production agency co-founder and business consultant. At Wedditors, I lead operations and post-production for wedding filmmakers and studios. Through Capture Camera, I work on commercial film production.

I also work with founders and creative teams on the business behind the work. That can mean refining an offer, improving client intake, automating follow-ups, or building a website or app that fits the way the team operates.
